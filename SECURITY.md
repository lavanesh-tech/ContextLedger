# Security policy

ContextLedger is a portfolio project, not a hosted service. Please report a
vulnerability privately through GitHub's **"Report a vulnerability"** button
(Security tab of this repository) rather than in a public issue.

Include the affected commit, steps to reproduce and the impact you expect. There is no
bug bounty. Reports are handled on a best-effort basis.

The review of the design, its controls and known open findings is in
[docs/SECURITY_REVIEW.md](docs/SECURITY_REVIEW.md).
